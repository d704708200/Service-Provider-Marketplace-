1. App Identity & Developer Information

This privacy policy applies exclusively to the MENDROO Customer mobile application (Android Package Name: com.serviceprovider.customerapp), hosted backend APIs (https://service-provider-marketplace.onrender.com), and associated customer services operated by MENDROO Technologies ("MENDROO", "we", "us", or "our").

2. Personal Information We Collect

Based on our direct code audit, we collect only the personal information required to authenticate customer accounts and fulfill on-demand service bookings:

  • Full Name: Collected during registration to identify your customer account and inform the assigned service technician of whom to contact upon arrival.
  • Mobile Phone Number: Unique primary contact key. Used for account authentication, password recovery, direct phone coordination with the assigned technician, and booking dispatch verification.
  • Email Address: Used for secure password reset verification, booking confirmation receipts, GST-compliant tax invoices, and account security notifications.
  • Password Hash: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using bcrypt (10 salt rounds) before database storage. Plaintext passwords are never logged, stored, or accessible to MENDROO employees.
  • Saved Delivery Addresses: Physical street address, building/flat number, locality, city, state, and zip code saved under your profile for quick selection during service checkout.
  • Profile Picture: Optional customer avatar image uploaded by the user, stored securely via encrypted cloud storage.

3. Device Location Data & Exact Purpose

The MENDROO Customer mobile application requests Android location permissions (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION):

  • How Location is Collected: Precise GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) are accessed strictly through the device's native location services when you tap "Use Current Location" or search for local services.
  • Purpose of Collection:
    1. To identify nearby verified service providers active within your coverage area.
    2. To pinpoint the exact service delivery destination for home/commercial technician dispatch.
    3. To accurately calculate travel distances and estimated technician arrival times.
  • No Background Location Tracking: The customer app does not collect background location while the app is closed, terminated, or not in active foreground use. Location access occurs only when actively interacting with location search or booking workflows.

4. Bookings, Service Requests & Customer Reviews

  • Service Scope Details: Selected category, service tag, preferred scheduling slot (instant on-demand dispatch or pre-scheduled appointment), service notes, and optional photos of the repair requirement.
  • Ratings & Reviews: Ratings (1 to 5 stars) and optional written reviews provided after job completion are displayed publicly to help maintain marketplace quality. Reviews remain anonymous regarding phone numbers and private addresses.

5. Payment Information & Razorpay Integration

MENDROO supports two transparent payment channels:

  1. Cash on Service (Cash after completion): You pay the service technician directly in cash upon satisfactory service delivery.
  2. Online Payments via Razorpay: When paying digitally for service fees, spare parts, or deposits, transactions are mediated directly by Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-licensed payment aggregator.
Zero Payment Card Storage: MENDROO does NOT collect, view, or store customer credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, expiry dates, UPI MPINs, or netbanking passwords. All digital payments are processed through Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway over TLS 1.3 encrypted sessions. Our servers only retain Razorpay transaction reference IDs (razorpay_order_id, razorpay_payment_id) for accounting and invoice generation.

7. Third-Party Sharing & Zero Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade customer personal data with data brokers, advertisers, or unaffiliated third parties. Data is shared strictly with the following operational entities:

  • Assigned Service Providers: Upon booking acceptance, your name, contact phone number, and service address are shared with the specific verified technician assigned to fulfill your job.
  • Razorpay: To process authorized digital payment transactions and refunds.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): To route encrypted transactional push notifications to your device.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Render (application hosting) and MongoDB Atlas (encrypted database storage).
  • Legal Mandates: Where required by valid judicial court orders, legal process, or regulatory tax inquiries under applicable Indian law.

8. Data Security & Storage Measures

  • In-Transit Encryption: All data exchanged between your mobile device and our backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • At-Rest Encryption: Database stores are hosted in ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certified cloud facilities with AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Access Controls: Administrative access is restricted via strict role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and IP whitelisting.

9. Google Play Account Deletion & User Rights

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, MENDROO provides simple, accessible mechanisms for complete account and data erasure:

Option A: Immediate In-App Deletion

1. Open the MENDROO Customer app.
2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom bar.
3. Under Legal & Privacy Policies, tap Delete Account.
4. Confirm your choice to permanently erase your profile and credentials immediately.

Option B: Web Account Deletion (Without Installing the App)

If you uninstalled the application or cannot access your phone, you can initiate immediate deletion via our public portal:

Open Customer Account Deletion Form →

Data Deletion Scope: Upon deletion, your name, email, phone number, saved addresses, profile photo, and device FCM tokens are permanently erased. Under statutory Indian tax laws (Goods and Services Tax Act), completed financial transaction logs and invoices are retained in an anonymized format for mandatory legal audit periods.

10. Children's Privacy

MENDROO Customer App is designed and marketed exclusively for individuals aged 18 years and older who have the legal capacity to enter into binding contracts. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.
