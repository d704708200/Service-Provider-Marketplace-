Customer Data Rights: In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and data protection regulations, users of MENDROO Customer have the full right to delete their accounts and request complete erasure of associated personal data.

Option 1: Immediate In-App Deletion

If you have the MENDROO Customer mobile app installed:

  1. Open the MENDROO Customer app.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Legal & Privacy Policies card.
  4. Tap Account Deletion Policy & Web Request (or in-app delete option) and confirm.
  5. Your account credentials, addresses, and personal records will be immediately and permanently erased.
