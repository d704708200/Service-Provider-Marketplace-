1. App Identity & Scope

This privacy policy applies exclusively to the MENDROO Provider mobile application (Android Package Name: com.mendroo.provider), the service provider web portal, associated administrative systems, and backend APIs operated by MENDROO Technologies.

2. Information We Collect from Service Providers

Based on our backend schema and API audit, registered Service Providers submit the following data:

  • Identity & Account Credentials: Full legal name, business name, mobile phone number, email address, profile avatar, and securely hashed password (bcrypt encryption with 10 salt rounds).
  • Professional Profile & Experience: Years of trade experience, primary service categories (e.g. Electrician, AC Repair, Plumbing), specific service tag offerings, and custom service pricing catalogs.
  • Availability & Working Hours: Weekly availability schedule (configured active days, working hours per day), and real-time online/offline toggle state (isAvailable).
  • Performance Records: Completed job counts, customer reviews, average rating scores, cancellation logs, and platform response time metrics.

3. Mandatory KYC Verification Documents (Aadhaar & PAN)

To ensure customer safety, prevent fraudulent registrations, and comply with marketplace aggregator compliance standards in India, MENDROO collects mandatory KYC verification records:

  • Aadhaar Card: Front and back images of the Aadhaar identity card. Stored in encrypted cloud storage with restricted administrative access.
  • PAN Card: Front and back images of Permanent Account Number (PAN) card for identity confirmation and income tax compliance.
  • Professional Service Certifications: Trade certificates, diplomas, or government vocational certificates verifying professional skills.
  • Usage & Access: KYC records are examined solely by authorized compliance administrators to determine verification status (verified, pending, or rejected). KYC documents are never displayed publicly to customers or third-party advertisers.

4. Location Data, Coverage Radii & Real-Time Dispatch

The MENDROO Provider application requests location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION):

  • Service Territory Definition: Providers specify central working coordinates and operational radius (in kilometers) to define their active service coverage area.
  • Real-Time Request Matching: When toggled "Online & Available", the app checks geographic proximity to broadcast incoming customer job requests situated within your designated radius.
  • Navigation to Job Site: Location coordinates guide the provider from current position to the customer's confirmed service address upon accepting a booking.
  • Foreground Operation: Location coordinates are captured during active application use and navigation workflows. No continuous background tracking takes place when the app is offline or closed.

5. In-App Wallet, Financial Payouts & Banking Data

For processing service earnings, commissions, and withdrawal payouts, we collect and manage:

  • Bank Account Credentials: Bank Account Number, Bank Name, IFSC Code, Account Holder Name, and UPI Virtual Payment Address (VPA).
  • Wallet Balances & Ledgers: Real-time available balance, pending withdrawal requests, completed withdrawal histories, platform commission deductions, and cash-on-service commission settlements.
  • Settlement Processor: Direct bank payouts are executed through secure banking partners and RBI-authorized payment processors (Razorpay).

7. Third-Party Sharing & Data Protection

We do not monetize, sell, or license provider personal data. Information is shared strictly under the following criteria:

  • To Matched Customers: Upon booking acceptance, your business name, mobile phone number, client rating, profile picture, and trade category are shared with the customer for fulfillment purposes.
  • Payment & Settlement Aggregators: Banking details and withdrawal amounts are submitted to authorized payment processors (Razorpay) for payout transfers.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Render (API hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), Cloudinary (document storage), and Google Firebase (push notifications).
  • Regulatory Authorities: Where compelled by law enforcement, Indian tax authorities, or court order regarding formal legal disputes.

8. Data Security Standards

All partner data, especially sensitive KYC identification and banking coordinates, are protected with enterprise-grade safeguards:

  • All API requests and image uploads are transmitted across encrypted TLS 1.3 / HTTPS connections.
  • Restricted document access: KYC images are secured in private repositories accessible only by authorized compliance personnel.
  • Strict password hashing using salted bcrypt algorithms.

9. Google Play Account Deletion & Partnership Termination

In accordance with Google Play Store User Data policies, Service Providers maintain the complete right to terminate their platform registration and request account erasure:

Option A: Immediate In-App Deletion

1. Open the MENDROO Provider app.
2. Navigate to the Profile tab in the bottom bar.
3. Under Legal & Policies, select Delete Account.
4. Confirm the permanent closure of your provider profile.

Option B: Web Account Deletion (Without Mobile Device)

If you uninstalled the application or switched devices, you can request immediate partner deactivation and data erasure through our public web portal:

Open Provider Account Deletion Form →

Erasure & Retention Scope: Upon verification, your profile, phone number, login credentials, uploaded KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN), and active service catalog will be permanently purged. In accordance with Section 139 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and GST statutory guidelines, completed financial ledgers, payout logs, and commission invoices are maintained in an anonymized format for statutory audit requirements.

10. Minors Strictly Prohibited

Service Providers must be at least 18 years of age and hold legal capacity to provide independent commercial services. Registration by minors is strictly prohibited and immediately terminated upon detection during KYC review.
