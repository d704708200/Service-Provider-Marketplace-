Provider Partnership Termination: In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and data privacy standards, registered Service Providers have the full right to terminate their platform partnership and request complete deletion of their account credentials and sensitive verification documents.

Option 1: Immediate In-App Deletion

If you have the MENDROO Provider mobile app installed:

  1. Open the MENDROO Provider app.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Legal & Policies section.
  4. Tap Account Deletion Policy & Web Request and confirm.
  5. Your provider account, service catalog, and KYC identity documents will be deactivated and scheduled for permanent erasure.

Data Erasure Scope for Service Partners

  • Permanently Purged: Uploaded Aadhaar & PAN images, skill certificates, bank account & UPI details, weekly working schedules, coverage radius coordinates, active broadcasts, and authentication credentials.
  • Wallet & Commission Balances: Any pending wallet withdrawal requests or unpaid platform commissions should be settled with the finance desk before final erasure.
  • Legally Mandated Records: Historical GST tax invoices and payment ledger entries are retained in an anonymized format under Indian Income Tax Act compliance guidelines.
